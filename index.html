THE PROCESS

From your first payment to your port.

Here is exactly how we work. Your vehicle is sourced and shipped through BE FORWARD, with tracking and documentation provided throughout the journey.

01

50% Advance Payment

You pay 50% of the vehicle amount upfront to confirm your order before shipment.

02

Shipment Booked

We apply for shipment and book cargo space for your vehicle.

03

B/L & Caplink Shared

Once shipment is booked, your B/L copy and Caplink are shared with you.

04

Track in Real Time

Use Caplink to monitor your vehicle's shipping progress from Japan to your port.

05

Remaining 50%

Approximately 10 days before arrival, you clear the remaining 50% balance.

06

Original Documents

After final payment, your original documents are sent by FedEx.

07

Vehicle Arrival

Your vehicle arrives at the destination port, documented and ready for clearance.
