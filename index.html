HOW IT WORKS

From Japan to Your Port

Here is exactly how we work, from your first payment to your vehicle arriving safely at your port.

01
PAYMENT

50% Advance Payment

Customer pays 50% of the vehicle amount upfront before shipment to confirm the order.

02
SHIPPING

Shipment Booked

Shipment is applied and booked, with cargo space arranged for your vehicle.

03
DOCUMENTATION

B/L & Caplink Shared

Receive the B/L copy and Caplink where shipping details are uploaded for tracking.

04
TRACKING

Track in Real Time

Use Caplink to monitor your vehicle's movement from Japan to your port.

05
PAYMENT

Remaining 50% Payment

Ten days before arrival, the customer clears the remaining 50% payment.

06
DOCUMENTS

Original Documents Sent

After final payment, original documents are sent via FedEx for port clearance.

07
ARRIVAL

Vehicle Arrival

Your vehicle arrives at the port, documented and ready for clearance.
